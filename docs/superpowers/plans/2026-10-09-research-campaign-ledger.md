## Prespecified eligibility and deterministic ranking

These are research screening thresholds, not evidence of statistical significance, profitability or live readiness. Freeze them in a versioned canonical protocol artifact before inspecting historical trade PnL; don't amend them after observing failures.

- Required: complete and independently reconciled phase evidence, consistent data/baseline/code and hypotheses across comparisons, no missing cost/coverage evidence. Raw funding provenance remains `observed_only`; diagnostic QA and any interval hypothesis remain visible. A valid diagnostic is not exchange certification.
- Each scenario must have at least100 completed training trades, at least30 in each training year, and at least50 completed validation trades. Each phase/scenario must cover at least5 of the approved pairs with a trade. Zero-trade/insufficient samples are ineligible, not winning by undefined denominators.
- Training2023 and2024 each have strictly positive total net PnL in both cost scenarios; validation2025 likewise. Aggregate mean realized net R must be positive in every phase/scenario. Net R = each trade's actual net PnL divided by its initial planned `risk_components_quote.total_stop_loss`, bound through its plan hash; never use gross R or planned target R as realized R.
- Baseline-cost profit factor must be at least1.2 for training and validation; adverse-cost PF at least1.0 (positive net criterion remains strict). PF uses positive/negative *net trade outcomes*, not gross price PnL. Zero loss denominator stays null with `no_negative_net_trades`; mark it ineligible for this screen rather than inventing infinity. Other undefined ratios retain explicit reasons.
- Marked maximum drawdown must not exceed6000 USDT (6% of the fixed initial balance) in any phase/scenario. This screen does not alter the kernel's stricter daily30 risk allowance. Use the kernel's marked drawdown, not closed-trade drawdown as a substitute; reports label both if computed.
- Eligible candidates rank by descending worst (minimum) mean realized net R across validation baseline/adverse, then lower worst validation marked drawdown, then fewer changed fields, then frozen catalogue order. No winner if none qualifies. Training eligibility alone never authorizes holdout.
