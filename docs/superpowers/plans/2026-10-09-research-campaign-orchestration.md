## Scope and safety

- New `python-orchestrator/app/backtesting/research/orchestration.py` and focused tests only; no edits to C1/C2 or published contracts. Parent supplies their final commits before integration. Synthetic tests only until parent explicitly launches the real workflow.
- Inputs are explicit absolute private dataset, instrument/cost/supplement roots, two existing training signal roots, two fresh validation roots, fresh registry/report/operational-evidence roots, frozen PHP appdir, and frozen B1 Python working directory/interpreter. No discovery of credentials, application DB, HTTP, exchange writes, setup edits, or holdout execution.
- Fixed approved ten-symbol priority, two five-symbol groups. Training source2022-11-01/score2023-01-01/end2025-01-01. Validation source2024-11-01/score2025-01-01/end2026-01-01. Diagnostic exclusive boundary is never scored. No configurable date/phase/universe bypass.
- Wait for both existing training reports to finish, with a finite explicit overall deadline. A missing report is pending, a failed/malformed report is fatal; never silently restart a failed training group. Polling intervals<=30 seconds and injectable clocks/sleep for tests. Do not inspect partial signals for outcome-driven decisions.
- When both training reports are complete and structurally match their fixed group/windows, launch at most two B1 validation subprocesses. Use the frozen B1 working directory, not this module's checkout: exact B1 code-path identity must match training. Sanitize subprocess environment using the existing allowlist convention; never pass secrets/dotenv/database URLs. Terminate only owned validation subprocesses on fatal failure/interrupt, retain their partial evidence, and do not auto-retry. Existing training processes are never signaled by this orchestrator.
- Refuse existing validation/registry/report/output targets before starting; no deletion, overwrite, hidden resume or fabricated completion. Record an immutable attempt before child launch; publish private0700/0600 atomic immutable terminal evidence on completion/failure. Bound diagnostic text. Allow parent to capture console logs separately.
