## Scope and next step

This evidence covers one cell on one dataset. It establishes neither profitability nor a general cause across other profiles or markets, and it does not prove a defect in the entry-zone calculation. A possible calculation-time versus candle-close ambiguity remains an unproven question, not a demonstrated cause or a reason to change behavior. Keep `1.1.0` byte-immutable and unchanged. An explicit profile choice is pending before any candidate is created; possible directions include EMA20/5m, VWAP tolerance, or other periods. No candidate or risk adjustment is authorized by this evidence. The implementation exposes the indicator field `ema_20`; any future `1.2.0` candidate must define its schema and whitelist explicitly in both PHP and Python.
